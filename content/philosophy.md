# Ramblings

I need a single term for a phenomena when humans use by-products of their
[umwelts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umwelt) to reason about principles
of reality completely orthogonal to the simplifications and metaphors imposed
by our sensory systems. By "sensory" I mean more broadly than our biological
senses - all kinds of human-specific information processing to which our minds
are adapted:

- cause-and-effect focused thinking (especially strong in western civilisations)
- anthropomorphisation of almost all things
- assuming there's a goal/reason behind things
- assuming there's an entity behind each goal
- assuming that entity is antrophomorphic


# Materialism/Substantialism

As far as I know, all dualists assume that the actions/influences of the
external (celestial, non-material) is impenetrable by the empirical, and yet
it influences the material. Dualists also assert that such system is causally
sound, obeying cause and effect. I believe such assertion to be unsound, for
it imposes a hard one-way barrier in the traceability of causes to effects -
as long as those causes come from the non-material, we cannot trace them back
to their source using the empirical apparatus. What follows such thinking is
that there exist influences on the material world which don't have traceable
beginnings, no matter how precise instruments you'll use trying to track them
down. To put it bluntly, such world model simply *does not compute* in my head.

My favorite argument against my materialist stance is simple I honestly endulge in thinking about it:

> Are numbers **real**?


# Free Will

> I don't have much to say about it because the arguments seem so clear-cut as
> to be almost uninteresting. Neurons do not fire themselves. [...] The switch
> cannot flip itself. QED.
>
> ~ Peter Watts, on free will - Echopraxia

People like Watts and Sapolsky are my core influences on this matter. I started
think of free will as a self-contradictory concept way back in high school,
this might have been my first identity crisis connected with a fixation on this
specific idea. I must admit that I cannot think about free will without some
nostalgia for those times.

Nevertheless, after assuming a materialist viewpoint, lack of free will follows
naturally. My favorite dualist counterpoint of this idea is a *radio receiver
metaphor* - the mind is simply **receiving** conciousness from some other plane
of non-material existance. My replica is the same as the one from the chapter
on Materialism - this assumes existence of things that can't be traced back to
their origin, but they somehow influence the material universe.

# Against the [Anthropic Principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic_principle)

This is gonna be one crazy metaphor, sorry about that. Do you know what plots
and charts are? They are a way to employ our visual cortex to analyze data which
does not have an actually visual origin. Imagine that this table of numbers is
a line, a bar, a pie - it's metaphor. An incredibly useful one, as it allows to
utilise the most efficient processing units of our brains to "crunch" non-visual
data **as if it were visual**. Now:

- replace "non-numerical data" with ["ontology"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology)
- replace "visual cortex" with ["theory of mind"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_mind)

...and you arrive at gods.

Our voyages into base principles of reality are actually projections into our
mammalian mode of thinking. This includes cause-and-effect orientation (which
is certainly useful), **but comes with intense anthropomorphisation** bolted
on top. We're not leaving our playground to look for universal truths, we're
actually burrying ourselves deeper into the sands of our own [mindware](/dukaj).

This is to me the poisoned spiraling, self-centered reasoning, essence of
religiousity. If there exists a capital-G *God*, the possible modes of its
existence which we have to consider are [so far detached](/cosmic-horror) from
our own, that I don't believe we can find any relief in faith.

Terms like ethics and redemption no longer have meaning in such comparison. We
gain no companion and no moral sign-posts.

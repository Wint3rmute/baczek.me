Notes on

my favorite  Polish writer.
[Official website](https://dukaj.pl/).

Dukaj uses a number of idiosyncratic concepts & terms, which I documented here.

# Concepts

**Post-Literate Culture** (*Cywilizacja postpiśmienna*) - civilisation which no longer uses text/literature/written word as its primary mean of communication. Instead of them it uses **direct experience transfer**.

**Direct Experience Transfer** (*Bezpośredni Transfer Przeżyć*) - means of
communication enabled by digital technology. Starting from radio/television,
through video chats, VR and social media, culminating in direct streams of thoughts
transmitted over brain implants or similar means.

**Mindware** (*Myślunek*) - bundle of cognitive habits and capacities that shapes an entire society, culture, politics, and morality. A "mode of thinking-and-perceiving", determined by used communication technologies. In Dukaj's theories, humans shifted from an *Oral Mindware* into *Literate Mindware* and are currently beginning the transition towards a *Direct Experience Transfer Mindware*.

# Terms

**Nolensum** (mix of latin terms) - overwhelming feeling of unwillingness, psychological state frequently occuring in post-scarsity civilisations. Close to depression, not the same. Cannot write a better definition at this point.

**In a half-blind** (*pöłzaślep*) - when a character augumented with a brain implant is using around half of their perception to interact with the digital world, only partially present in reality. Examples: characters piloting complex machines, characters discussing and gathering related data at the same time.

**In a full blind** (*pełny zaślep*) - next step from being in a *half-blind* - allocating the entirety of your perception apparatus to the digital realms. Deep work/research, computer games.
